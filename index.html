DroneOps: Aerial Survey and Fleet Management System

DroneOps keeps an organisation's drone work in one place. Pilots request flights, technicians record the condition of every drone, a dispatcher approves or denies each flight request, and a safety officer watches damage reports and pilot licenses.

Pages

Account

  • Login - log in as a pilot, technician or dispatcher
  • Pilot Registration - create a pilot account with license details

Pilot

  • Pilot Dashboard - flight hours, upcoming flights and profile
  • New Flight Plan - request a flight
  • My Flights - status of submitted requests

Technician

  • Drone Grid - every drone and its status
  • Maintenance Update - record repairs, battery use and damage

Dispatcher

  • Flight Schedule - approve or deny flight requests
  • Fleet Management - add and remove drones
  • User Management - create and remove accounts

Safety officer

  • Safety Dashboard - fleet readiness, damage reports and license compliance
  • Grounding & Damage Audit - review grounded or damaged drones
  • Pilot Compliance - pilot licenses and flight hours
  • Compliance Notices - send notices to pilots and crew
